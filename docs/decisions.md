## 2026-05-20  Experiment A — k-sweep + coordinate-mask comparison

- **Goal**: probe whether the Phase 0A curvature signal (inversion on q/o_proj, agreement on mlp.down_proj at k=64) is robust to (a) rank choice and (b) the choice of "principal" operator (SVD-subspace projection vs. the source paper's `M_princ = Top_α(|W₀^(k)|)` coordinate mask).
- **Implementation**: reuses Phase 0A's cached ΔW, earlier checkpoint, minibatch, and NLL proxy loss. Adds `scripts/experiment_a_ksweep.py` (full-SVD cache + direction sweep) and `scripts/summarize_experiment_a.py` (table + sign-flip grid). Driver: `scripts/run_experiment_a.sh`. Outputs under `results/public_pair_deepscaler/experiment_a/`.
- **Direction set per layer**: realized (k-independent), random matched-Frobenius × 3 seeds (k-independent), subspace princ+comp × {16,64,256,512}, coord-mask princ+comp × {16,64,256,512} × α ∈ {0.05, 0.30, 0.50}. 36 directions × 3 minibatches × 3 layers = 324 HVPs; wall clock ~ 90 s on one L40S after model load.
- **α choice**: paper uses α=0.50 in its sparse-FT experiments; we add 0.30 and 0.05 to probe whether stricter "principal-only" masks change the curvature signature.
- **Numerical reproducibility**: subspace operator at k=64 reproduces every Phase 0A direction's mean curvature to ≥ 4 sig figs, confirming the new pipeline is computing the same HVP.
- **Headline finding**: the curvature inversion is **layer-dependent**, **operator-dependent**, and (for mlp.down_proj) **k-dependent**. q_proj inverts at every (operator, k, α). o_proj inverts under subspace at every k but the coord-mask operator restores the paper's ordering at k≤64 + α≥0.30. mlp.down_proj's Phase 0A "matches paper" finding at k=64 reverses at k=256 and k=512 even under the same operator — that confirmation should not be carried forward. Detailed table in `docs/notes.md` under "Experiment A".
- **Decision**: do not treat the Phase 0A mlp.down_proj result as supporting evidence for Gate II's paper-strength reading. Treat the q_proj inversion as the only robust per-layer signal at this scale; treat o_proj as nuanced (operator-conditioned). Both are consistent with CLAUDE.md Hypotheses 2 and 3. Increase minibatch count before publishing any per-layer claim.

## 2026-05-21  Noise-floor flag + HVP module refactor

- **Why**: a 35-cell sweep (Experiment A's grid was 48 cells already; depth/matrix sweeps will be larger) is illegible without an explicit "this cell is in the noise" marker, and reloading a 1.5B fp32 checkpoint per layer is a multi-minute waste of I/O. Both are now fixed before the next experiment.
- **Noise floor**: a cell in the sign-flip grid is now flagged `is_undetermined_2sigma = True` iff `|princ_mean − comp_mean| < 2 · std_paired(per_minibatch_(P − C))`. Paired std (not unpaired) because P and C share the same minibatch and the bulk of their per-mb noise cancels. Strict small-n alternative `is_undetermined_strict_t` uses `t_{0.975, n−1} · SE` with a hard-coded t-table to avoid a scipy dependency. Both flags are persisted in `summary.json`; the sign-flip plot maps undetermined cells to white via `cells[i,j] = 0` and stamps `[u]` plus the z-score in the label.
- **Consequence for current numbers**: at n=3 minibatches, 42 of 48 Experiment A cells are undetermined under both rules. The 6 surviving cells are all *inversion* (P<C); zero "paper-matching" cells survive. The Phase 0A claim that q_proj's inversion is uniformly robust must be weakened to "directionally consistent but currently within noise"; the only decisive signals come from o_proj subspace at large k and mlp.down_proj coord-mask at large k + small α. Detailed table in `docs/notes.md`.
- **Refactor**: `_hvp.py` now owns `load_model_for_hvp`, `freeze_all_but`, `loss_fn`, `directional_curvature`, `matched_norm_random`. Both `directional_curvature.py` (Phase 0A driver) and `experiment_a_ksweep.py` import from it. Phase 0A's `main` now loads the model once outside the layer loop. Verified by bit-exact reproduction of every per-minibatch curvature value across both pipelines.
- **Next**: before publishing or starting Experiment C, raise n_minibatches from 3 to 8–16. The estimator-variance ceiling is currently the bottleneck, not the model or operator definition.
